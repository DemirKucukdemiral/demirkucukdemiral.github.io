Autonomous Vehicle Simulator

Development of a high-fidelity autonomous vehicle simulation environment for control and decision-making research.

Objectives

The project investigates vehicle dynamics, path tracking, autonomous control and decision-making in simulation.

Technical Approach

  • Nonlinear vehicle dynamics
  • Gazebo simulation
  • C++
  • Model Predictive Control
  • ROS 2
